Sunwave Home Appliances Co., Ltd. is a professional small-appliance manufacturer based in Ningbo, Zhejiang — the heart of China's home-appliance export industry. We specialize in four product lines: **kitchen appliances**, **home environment appliances**, **personal care** and **floor care**.

Our campus houses the complete production chain: injection molding, motor and PCB assembly, plastic spraying, packaging and a reliability lab that runs life-cycle, drop, humidity and burn-in tests on every new model. This vertical integration is why we can hold consistent quality across large batches while keeping lead times at 25–35 days.

For our OEM/ODM partners we offer everything from logo printing and color customization to full industrial design, market-specific certification (CE, RoHS, ETL, FDA, GS) and retail-ready packaging development. Tell us the market you are entering — we will bring the engineering, the compliance and the manufacturing capacity.
